About Counselling

Are you feeling anxious, low, stressed, or emotionally overwhelmed? There are times when life can feel difficult, exhausting, or isolating, particularly when you are unsure where to turn. Challenging experiences can bring feelings of anxiety, sadness, anger, fear, stress, or emotional pain. Counselling can offer a safe and supportive space to pause, reflect, and begin to make sense of what you are experiencing, at a pace that feels comfortable for you.

I offer a confidential, non-judgemental space where you can talk openly and honestly about whatever may be troubling you. You do not need to have all the answers, and you do not have to face everything on your own. Sometimes, simply having someone listen with genuine understanding can be an important first step towards feeling heard, gaining clarity, and finding a way forward.

My approach is calm, compassionate, and respectful. I will meet you where you are and support you in exploring your thoughts, feelings, and experiences without judgement or pressure. Together, we can work towards greater self-understanding, emotional balance, and a stronger sense of wellbeing, whatever challenges you may be facing.

Welcome to my counselling practice.

I am a Person-Centred, Solution Focused, and NET (Narrative Exposure Therapy) counsellor with nine years of full-time experience supporting people across a wide spectrum of needs, including neurodiverse individuals and people from diverse and marginalised backgrounds.

I am a member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP), and I place your wellbeing, safety, and best interests at the heart of every session.

I am committed to ongoing professional development, ensuring that my practice remains ethical, inclusive, and responsive to your individual needs.

Having grown up in Morocco, I bring a culturally sensitive, respectful, and open perspective to our work together. I aim to create a safe, non-judgemental space where you can feel heard, understood, and accepted, at your own pace.
